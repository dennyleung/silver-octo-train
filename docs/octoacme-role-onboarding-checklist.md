# OctoAcme Role Onboarding Checklist

Use this checklist when a new team member joins a project in any of the defined roles. It helps them understand the project context, their responsibilities, and how to collaborate effectively with the team from day one.

---

## General Onboarding (All Roles)

Complete these items regardless of role:

- [ ] Read the [Project Management Overview](octoacme-project-management-overview.md)
- [ ] Read the [Roles & Personas](octoacme-roles-and-personas.md) doc to understand all team roles
- [ ] Review the [RACI Template](octoacme-raci-template.md) for this project to understand your ownership areas
- [ ] Review the [Stakeholder Mapping](octoacme-stakeholder-mapping.md) to know who the key stakeholders are
- [ ] Review the [Escalation Path](octoacme-escalation-path.md) so you know how to raise blockers
- [ ] Review the [Decision Log](octoacme-decision-log.md) for past key decisions on this project
- [ ] Get access to project tools (project board, repo, communication channels)
- [ ] Meet with the Project Manager for a 30-minute orientation call
- [ ] Be introduced to the team (standup, Slack, or team meeting)
- [ ] Review the project charter or one-pager for goals, scope, and timeline

## Role-Specific Checklists

### Project Sponsor
- [ ] Approve or confirm the project charter and budget
- [ ] Review and confirm the stakeholder map for executive stakeholders
- [ ] Confirm escalation path and decision authority with the Project Manager
- [ ] Agree on the cadence for executive briefings (monthly / milestone-based)
- [ ] Review the risk register for any existing high-priority risks

### Product Manager
- [ ] Review the product vision, roadmap, and OKRs
- [ ] Meet with the Product Owner to align on backlog priorities
- [ ] Review existing feature specs and acceptance criteria in the backlog
- [ ] Confirm stakeholder engagement approach with the Project Manager
- [ ] Schedule a weekly alignment session with the Project Manager

### Product Owner
- [ ] Review and take ownership of the product backlog
- [ ] Confirm sprint cadence and ceremony schedule with the Agile Coach / Scrum Master
- [ ] Meet with Developers to review the top backlog items
- [ ] Confirm acceptance criteria format and "Definition of Done" with the Quality Lead
- [ ] Review the [RACI Template](octoacme-raci-template.md) to confirm backlog and sprint ownership

### Project Manager
- [ ] Review all existing project documentation (charter, plan, risk register, decision log)
- [ ] Update or confirm the [RACI Template](octoacme-raci-template.md) for the project
- [ ] Review and update the [Stakeholder Mapping](octoacme-stakeholder-mapping.md)
- [ ] Confirm communication cadence with the team and stakeholders
- [ ] Set up or review the project board and status reporting process
- [ ] Schedule kickoff or re-orientation meeting if joining mid-project

### Agile Coach / Scrum Master
- [ ] Review the team's current agile practices and delivery metrics (velocity, cycle time)
- [ ] Confirm ceremony schedule (standup, planning, review, retrospective)
- [ ] Review the impediment log for any outstanding blockers
- [ ] Meet with the Product Owner to review backlog health
- [ ] Agree on "Definition of Done" criteria with the team and Quality Lead

### Quality Lead
- [ ] Review the existing test strategy (or create one if absent)
- [ ] Review the "Definition of Done" and confirm quality gate criteria
- [ ] Review the backlog for testability of acceptance criteria
- [ ] Set up or review the defect tracking process
- [ ] Meet with Developers to align on test automation approach
- [ ] Confirm UAT process and stakeholder involvement with the Project Manager

### Developer
- [ ] Set up local development environment and confirm access to all repos
- [ ] Review the top backlog items and confirm understanding of acceptance criteria
- [ ] Review coding standards, PR process, and branch strategy
- [ ] Meet with the Quality Lead to understand the test strategy and Definition of Done
- [ ] Attend first standup and sprint planning session

### Stakeholder
- [ ] Review the project charter or one-pager for goals, scope, and timeline
- [ ] Confirm your role and expectations with the Project Manager
- [ ] Confirm communication preferences (frequency, channel, level of detail)
- [ ] Review the stakeholder map to understand other stakeholders involved
- [ ] Schedule your first sprint review attendance with the Project Manager

---

## First Week Goals (All Roles)

By the end of your first week, you should be able to answer:

1. What is the project trying to achieve, and what is the current status?
2. Who are the key people I work with, and what are their roles?
3. What are my top responsibilities and decision rights?
4. How do I raise a blocker or escalate an issue?
5. Where are the key project artifacts (backlog, risk register, decision log)?
