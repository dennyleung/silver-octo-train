# OctoAcme Stakeholder Mapping Template

Use this template at project initiation to identify stakeholders, assess their influence and interest, and plan engagement accordingly. Update throughout the project as the stakeholder landscape changes.

---

## How to Use

1. List all individuals or groups who have an interest in or are impacted by the project.
2. Rate each stakeholder's **Influence** (ability to affect the project) and **Interest** (degree to which they care about outcomes) on a Low / Medium / High scale.
3. Determine the appropriate engagement strategy based on the quadrant below.
4. Assign an owner to manage each stakeholder relationship.
5. Review and update at each major milestone.

---

## Engagement Strategy by Quadrant

```
High Influence │  MANAGE CLOSELY     │  KEEP SATISFIED   │
               │  (High Interest)    │  (Low Interest)   │
───────────────┼─────────────────────┼───────────────────┤
Low Influence  │  KEEP INFORMED      │  MONITOR          │
               │  (High Interest)    │  (Low Interest)   │
               └─────────────────────┴───────────────────┘
                     High Interest          Low Interest
```

| Quadrant | Strategy |
|---|---|
| **High Influence / High Interest** | Engage frequently; involve in key decisions; manage closely. |
| **High Influence / Low Interest** | Keep satisfied with summary updates; involve for major decisions. |
| **Low Influence / High Interest** | Keep well-informed; gather feedback; leverage as advocates. |
| **Low Influence / Low Interest** | Monitor; provide general updates; don't over-invest. |
